Scoring System

Tournament Scoring Rules

How fantasy points are calculated across match outcomes, hole-by-hole performance, and penalties.

Match Play

  • Match Victory +10 pts
  • Match Halved (Tie) +5 pts
  • Hole Won +1 pt
  • Winning Margin e.g. 2&1 or 2 UP = +2 pts
    +1 / hole

Shots & Bonuses

  • Birdie +3 pts
  • Eagle +8 pts
  • Holed from Off Green +5 pts
  • Hole-in-One / Albatross +15 pts

Penalties

  • Hazard / Out of Bounds Water, lost ball, OB stroke
    -2 pts

Foursomes Format Note

In Foursomes (Round 2), all shot-based scoring bonuses (Birdies, Eagles, Holed from Off Green) apply to both players on the team.
